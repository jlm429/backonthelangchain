## Warehouse Scanner Synchronization

Classification: Fictional demo knowledge.

Question: My Acme warehouse scanner is not showing the latest pick list.

Answer: Place the scanner in its cradle, open StockFlow, and choose Sync Device.
Keep the scanner docked until the green SYNCED banner appears. If the same batch
remains stale, record the scanner asset tag and contact Warehouse Systems Support.
