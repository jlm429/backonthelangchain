## Field VPN Certificate Recovery

Classification: Fictional demo knowledge.

Question: The Acme Field VPN says my device certificate is missing or expired.

Answer: Connect to the Acme Guest network, open Device Trust Center, and select
Renew Field Certificate. Wait for the certificate state to show ACTIVE before
reconnecting to Field VPN. If renewal fails, contact Network Access Support with
the Device Trust request ID.
